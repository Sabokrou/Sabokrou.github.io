Join the research group

I welcome motivated people interested in trustworthy machine learning, anomaly detection, AI safety, and robust computer vision.

Opportunities

Potential roles include research collaborators, research assistants, interns, postdoctoral researchers, and visiting researchers.

Current opportunities: funded and volunteer research-assistant or internship opportunities may be available depending on the project and host institution.

How to apply

Applications for advertised calls are managed through our secure recruitment portal. Choose an open call and submit the form—no account or sign-in is required:

  • Your CV
  • A short description of your research interests and relevant skills
  • Selected publications, code, or project links, if available
  • The role and preferred starting period
View calls and apply online

Upload your CV and save the private tracking link shown after submission to check your status and interview details. Contact sabokro@gmail.com for corrections. Applications are accepted only during each call’s stated dates.

For collaboration inquiries outside an advertised call, contact me by email.

Website information

Updated 14 October 2026

This website presents my research, publications, teaching, academic service, writing, and industry consulting.

Hosting and external resources

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Pages use Google Fonts, and the consulting pages load icons from the Cloudflare CDN. Requests to these services are handled under their own privacy policies. See GitHub’s privacy statement, Google Fonts privacy information, and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The academic pages do not include Google Analytics, advertising, or Disqus comment scripts. External websites linked here may have their own cookies and data practices.

Contact and applications

Email links open your email application. Information you choose to send is received through the relevant email provider.

Advertised research opportunities use a separate recruitment portal. Application responses and uploaded CVs are handled by that service for the recruitment process. This page does not describe that service’s storage or retention arrangements; consult the information in the portal before submitting.

Teaching materials and other content

The teaching page links to published student course materials. Follow the attribution, license, and usage information provided with each document. Linked papers, code, photographs, and other third-party materials retain their own licenses.

Before you begin

Have your own email inbox and your six-digit university student ID ready. You can use an email you already own; the teaching team does not need to know it in advance. Keep using the same email for this course.

The email code confirms that you control your inbox. Your student ID identifies your course record. They are two different things.

First visit: register and request access

  1. Open the results page

    On the course website, go to Course grades and choose Open grades, then Open course results. You can also use the button above.

  2. Enter your email

    Type an email address whose inbox you can access and click Send code. Use your own email, not a friend's or the TA's.

  3. Confirm your email

    Open the sign-in email. Copy its one-time code into Code from your email and click Verify code. Check your spam folder if needed. Do not enter your student ID in this field.

  4. Enter your student ID

    On Connect your student record, enter your six-digit ID exactly, including any leading zero. Click Request connection. You do not need a separate invitation code or password.

  5. Wait for approval

    You should see Waiting for approval. The instructor or TA must confirm that this email belongs to the student whose ID was entered. If asked, confirm your identity directly with the teaching team. Your scores stay hidden until approval.

  6. Check your results

    After the teaching team approves your request, click Check approval status. Your name, student ID, and published results should appear. If you have signed out, sign in again using the same email.

Later visits: sign in and refresh

  1. Open the results page and use the same email you connected previously.
  2. If asked to sign in, click Send code and verify the new code from your inbox. A saved session may take you straight to your results.
  3. Click Refresh results to check for newly published grades. You do not need to submit your ID again after approval.
  4. Click Sign out when using a shared computer.

Understand your scores

  • Awaiting publication: the teaching team has not released this result. It is not a posted zero.
  • Published course points: the weighted points from results released so far, out of 100. This is not your final grade while results are missing.
  • Weekly labs: the best nine of 12 labs contribute 15 course points. Individual labs can show Pass, Revise, Fail, and an optional numeric score.
  • Exams and assignments: each published score contributes according to the weight displayed.
  • Progress and course grade band: read the accompanying note. A final course band is shown only after all results are released.

You can view your own published results; you cannot change grades or view classmates' results. The university's official record remains authoritative.

If something does not work

I did not receive the email code.

Check the email spelling and your inbox and spam folders. Wait briefly, then request a new code if needed. Use the most recent code. If sending is temporarily limited, wait before trying again.

My code is invalid or expired.

Request a new code and enter it with the same email address. Use the code from the email, not your student ID. Do not share codes with anyone.

I entered the wrong student ID.

Do not submit someone else's ID. If your request is pending, ask the teaching team to reject it. You can then submit your correct ID. A connected record cannot be changed from this page; contact the teaching team.

My request is pending or was not approved.

Pending means the teaching team has not finished checking your request. Click Check approval status later. If it was rejected, check your ID and contact the teaching team before submitting again.

I can sign in, but no grades are shown.

If approval is pending, wait for approval first. If your record is connected, the teaching team may not have published any results yet. Click Refresh results after a release is announced.

I need to use a different email, or I see the wrong name.

Contact the teaching team. Do not try to claim another record. There is currently no self-service email or student-ID change on the results page.

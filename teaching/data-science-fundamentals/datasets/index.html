Responsible use

Before analysing

  • State what one row represents before counting or joining.
  • Never average an identifier such as student_id.
  • Treat age, study mode, and first-generation status as sensitive attributes.
  • Use sensitive attributes for fairness auditing, not to allocate or withhold opportunities.

Mathematical conventions

All training data, bags and factory examples are synthetic. Probabilities depend on the stated model. An event with probability zero cannot be used as a conditioning denominator in these finite examples. In a continuous model, individual points can have probability zero while nearby intervals have positive probability.

The fair-die simulation uses the same MT19937 integer seeding and rejection sampling as Python random.Random(seed).randint(1,6) for non-negative 32-bit integer seeds. Seed 9 reproduces the lecture counts. Weighted draws use inverse-CDF sampling from the same generator.

Normal interval probabilities use a numerical error-function approximation, accurate to about 10−7 in absolute probability. Plotted curves use finite samples. The numerical probability comes from the CDF, rather than from counting pixels.
